### How Is Daily Production Calculated?

For example, the 8-head configuration has a stated maximum speed of 80 bottles per minute when filling water-based products.

80 bottles × 60 minutes = 4,800 bottles per hour.

4,800 bottles × 8 hours = 38,400 bottles per shift.

Therefore, the theoretical maximum production capacity is 38,400 bottles during an 8-hour shift.

Actual production may vary depending on product viscosity, filling volume, bottle size, material flow, conveyor settings and operating interruptions.

The stated water-based production speeds should not be assumed for thick products such as peanut butter, chocolate spread or heavy pastes.
