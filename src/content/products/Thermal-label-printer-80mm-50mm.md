### Printer Compatibility: Check Before Ordering

These are **direct thermal labels**, so they must be used with a printer that supports direct thermal label media and accepts the 80mm label width.

Before purchasing, check the following:

- Your printer supports direct thermal printing rather than thermal transfer only.
- The printer accepts labels with a width of 80mm and a height of 50mm.
- The roll's core diameter, outer diameter and label-gap configuration match your printer.
- Your printing software supports the required label dimensions and barcode layout.

Please note that an 80mm label width does not guarantee compatibility with every 80mm thermal receipt printer. Some printers have narrower media guides or do not support adhesive label stock.

### Direct Thermal Labels vs Thermal Transfer Labels

Direct thermal and thermal transfer labels use different printing methods. Direct thermal labels react to heat from the printhead, whereas thermal transfer printing uses a ribbon to transfer ink onto suitable label material.

Choose direct thermal labels when your printer supports this media type and you want to print without a ribbon. For applications requiring longer-lasting labels or greater resistance to heat, sunlight, abrasion or chemicals, assess whether a suitable thermal transfer label is more appropriate.

Actual durability depends on the label coating, storage conditions and intended application.
