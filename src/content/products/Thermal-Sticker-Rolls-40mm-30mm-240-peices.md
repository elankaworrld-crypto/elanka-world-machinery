The 40mm x 30mm label format is useful when you only need a small area for product names, short descriptions, stock references or identification codes. The amount of information you can print depends on the printer resolution, font size and label design.

Direct thermal printers use heat-sensitive label material instead of ink cartridges. This makes them convenient for simple portable labelling tasks without the need to replace printing ink.
